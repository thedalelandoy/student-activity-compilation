For agency owners who send cold email

Your website is the second email.

A good cold email gets them to Google your name. What they find next decides whether they reply.

your agency

What happens after they read it

Nobody replies to a stranger. They check you first, and your site either finishes the email's job or undoes it.

  1. 01

    The email sends them to Google.

    They want to know who you are before they reply.

  2. 02

    A messy site undoes it.

    Clutter, stock photos and "Welcome to our website" say nothing about them.

  3. 03

    A clear one backs it up.

    The site says the same thing the email did, so the reply is "Sure, let's talk."

Check your site before you send.

Four things to fix before the first email goes out. Each one takes an afternoon, not a redesign.

  1. One clear line on top.

    Say who you help and what you do for them. Skip "Welcome to our website."

  2. Show proof that fits.

    Case studies from their kind of business, not a wall of every logo you've worked with.

  3. One next step.

    A single button that matches the email's ask, so nobody has to guess where to click.

  4. Check it on a phone.

    They'll probably Google you from their phone, between meetings.
