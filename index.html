About me

Hi, I'm Dale Landoy, a BS Marketing student at Mapúa Malayan Colleges Laguna and the founder of Casual Outbounds, a B2B cold outbound agency. I took a gap year to build the agency, and now I'm in school learning the craft behind the websites my clients and I depend on.

What I learned

  • Structure first. Semantic elements like header, nav, main, section, article and footer make a page easier to read for people, screen readers and search engines.
  • Styling as a system. Keeping colors, type and spacing in CSS variables makes three different pages feel like one body of work.
  • Layout that adapts. Flexbox, CSS Grid and media queries let one page rearrange itself for a phone, a tablet and a desktop without scrolling sideways.
  • Small details matter. Alt text, relative paths and links that actually work are what separate a page that looks finished from one that is.
